Build a polished, responsive web app for me and my two friends to track shared expenses and repayments between the three of us.

## Core Problem

There are 3 people who regularly hang out and spend money together.

We often don't settle expenses immediately. One person might pay the entire bill, and the other two pay that person back later.

Example:

* Total expense: $150
* Person A paid the entire $150
* The expense is split equally between A, B, and C
* Therefore B owes A $50 and C owes A $50
* Later B sends A $50
* Later C sends A $50
* Everyone is settled and the final balances should all be $0

The app should keep track of **both expenses and repayments** and determine exactly who currently owes whom and how much.

---

# Main Goal

The app should answer one question instantly:

**"After accounting for every expense and every repayment, who needs to pay whom, and how much?"**

It should calculate the mathematically correct balances and simplify the debts so the minimum practical number of payments is required.

# 5. Accounting Logic

Implement the accounting carefully.

For every expense:

1. The person who paid gets credit for the full amount they paid.
2. Every participant gets charged their assigned share.
3. Therefore:

Net balance = amount personally paid - person's share of expenses

Then incorporate repayments:

If B pays A $50:

* B's net position increases by $50
* A's net position decreases by $50

At the end:

* Positive balances represent people who should receive money.
* Negative balances represent people who need to pay money.
* The sum of all balances must always equal approximately $0, allowing only for minor floating-point rounding.

Example:

Expense:
A pays $150
A/B/C split equally

Before repayments:

A = +100
B = -50
C = -50

Then:

B → A $50

Balances:

A = +50
B = 0
C = -50

Then:

C → A $50

Balances:

A = 0
B = 0
C = 0

Show:

**Everyone is settled ✓**

# 26. Settlement Algorithm Requirement

This part is extremely important.

Do NOT calculate settlement by simply looking at each expense independently.

First calculate:

**Net balance for each person**

Then separate:

* Creditors: positive balance
* Debtors: negative balance

Match debtors against creditors until all balances reach zero.

Try to minimize the number of settlement transactions.

For example:

A +$100
B +$50
C -$150

Should produce:

C → A $100
C → B $50

rather than generating unnecessary intermediate payments.

Make sure the algorithm works when there are multiple creditors and multiple debtors.

Use cents throughout the calculations to avoid floating-point errors.
